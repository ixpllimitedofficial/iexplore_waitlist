# iExplore Platform Documentation

## Platform Description

iExplore is a dynamic and innovative mobile and web platform focused on redefining nightlife experiences in Africa. It connects users (explorers) to the best nightlife spots in their city, including clubs, bars, lounges, and event venues. The platform provides seamless discovery, connection, and engagement with nightlife locations, offering unique experiences, discounts, and up-to-date information.

## Features

- **Spot Discovery:** Find and explore trending nightlife spots, clubs, bars, and lounges nearby.
- **Event Updates:** Stay in the loop with the latest events and happenings in your city.
- **Reviews & Ratings:** Share and read reviews and ratings for venues and experiences.
- **Discounts & Offers:** Access exclusive discounts on drinks and special offers at partner venues.
- **Vendor Dashboard:** Nightlife businesses can manage their profiles, showcase offerings, run ads, and track engagement.
- **User Profiles:** Users can create accounts, save favorite spots, and share experiences.
- **Advertising:** Businesses can boost ads, promote events, and reach targeted audiences.
- **Referral System:** Track referrals and influencer campaigns across social platforms.
- **Content Moderation:** Admins can verify spots, moderate content, and manage platform quality.

## Target Audience

- **Nightlife Enthusiasts:** Individuals seeking new places to enjoy nightlife, events, and social gatherings.
- **Vendors/Businesses:** Clubs, bars, lounges, and event organizers looking to attract and engage customers.
- **Influencers & Promoters:** Social media personalities and promoters who drive traffic and engagement.
- **Admins:** Platform managers overseeing content, user activity, and business onboarding.

## How It Works & User Journey

### For Users
1. **Sign Up/Login:** Register via the app or website.
2. **Discover Spots:** Browse and search for venues and events based on location and preferences.
3. **View Details:** Access spot details, reviews, ratings, and available offers.
4. **Engage:** Share experiences, rate venues, and connect with other users.
5. **Save Favorites:** Bookmark preferred spots for future visits.
6. **Get Discounts:** Redeem offers and discounts at partner venues.

### For Vendors
1. **Business Onboarding:** Register and create a vendor profile.
2. **Spot Management:** Add and manage venue details, offerings, and operation hours.
3. **Run Ads:** Create and boost ads to reach more users.
4. **Track Engagement:** Monitor ratings, reviews, and user traffic.
5. **Showcase Offers:** Highlight special deals and events to attract customers.

### For Admins
1. **Content Moderation:** Review and verify new spots and user-generated content.
2. **User Management:** Oversee user onboarding, support, and activity.
3. **Vendor Management:** Approve and support business onboarding and ad campaigns.

## Platform Flow
- **Landing Page:** Introduction, brand story, and call-to-action for users and vendors.
- **User Dashboard:** Personalized feed of spots, events, and recommendations.
- **Vendor Dashboard:** Tools for business management, analytics, and advertising.
- **Admin Panel:** Controls for moderation, analytics, and platform management.

## Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** React, Next.js, Tailwind CSS, ShadCN
- **State Management:** Zustand
- **Forms & Validation:** React-Hook-form, Zod
- **Animations:** AOS

## Additional Information
- **Mobile-first Design:** Fully responsive and optimized for mobile devices.
- **Secure Accounts:** User authentication and data privacy.
- **Community-driven:** Reviews, ratings, and social engagement drive platform value.
